# 01 — Acid Editorial

Poster-language system for a branding studio. Acid yellow on paper stock, condensed grotesque shouting, serif italic answering back. Reference: @estudiomona.co feed.

## Principles
- **One acid, one paper.** Max two background colours per set. Black is structure, not a field.
- **Type is the image.** Headlines fill the frame edge to edge. Empty layouts mean small type, not short copy.
- **Two voices per post.** A shouted grotesque statement plus one italic serif aside. The aside carries personality.
- **Cut, don't render.** Halftones, scans, cut-out objects, sticker edges. Nothing gradient-smooth or 3D.

## Colour
| Role | Hex | Use | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acid | `#E8E559` | Field, highlight | 60% |
| Paper | `#F2F0E6` | Field | 25% |
| Ink | `#111111` | Type, rules, panels | 12% |
| Cut white | `#FFFFFF` | Sticker edges, cut-outs | 3% |
| Rare blue | `#5B87B8` | One tile per nine-grid | — |

Legal: ink on acid, acid on ink, ink on paper. Illegal: white type on acid, acid type on paper.

## Typography
- **Display** — Archivo 900 / 700, uppercase, letter-spacing −4.5% at hero size, line-height .86–.94.
- **Aside** — Instrument Serif italic only. Interjections, questions, parentheticals.
- **Meta** — Space Mono, 9–13px, letter-spacing .14–.20em, uppercase. Handles, credits, category tags.

Scale (1080×1080 frame): hero 120–190px · statement 64–90px · aside 60–80px · body 30–36px · meta 20–26px.

## Components
- **Highlight block** — acid marker behind one phrase, 6px horizontal bleed. One per layout.
- **Rule underline** — 4px, offset 4px, on the pivot word.
- **Ink tag** — black box, acid mono text, bottom corner. Never centred.
- **Sticker aside** — boxed italic serif, 2px border, rotated 1–2°, white fill.
- **Dot-leader annotation** — 7px dot, hairline connector, underlined phrase. Hand-placed, scattered.
- **Ink panel** — solid black block over photography, max one per layout.

## Layout
- 1:1 frames, safe margin 6%. Headlines may bleed off one edge only.
- Handle top-centre or bottom-left in mono, always letterspaced.
- Nine-grid mix: 4 acid, 3 paper, 2 photo. Max one blue.

## Imagery
Grainy black-and-white photography, obsolete objects (floppy disks, air fresheners, balloons), phone-mirror selfies with sticker overlays, halftone hands. Colour photography only when the subject is already yellow-adjacent.

## Voice
Provocative questions in Portuguese, second person, short. A statement then a doubt: "Querer ser autêntico todo mundo quer… mas, tu bancas essa escolha?"
