# 06 — Desk Collage

Advice-and-strategy system built like a physical desk: torn paper taped to moody photography, checklists, hand annotations, one circled phrase per frame. Reference: @olyacooper marketing grid.

## Principles
- **Statement, then proof.** One claim in bold caps plus a small lowercase support line. Never two claims.
- **One handmade mark.** A circle, arrow, asterisk, or handwritten note — exactly one per frame.
- **Colour carries the key word.** Ember for urgency, teal for calm nuance. One coloured word per headline, never both.
- **Objects, not people.** Desks, cameras, candles, laptops in low light. People appear in profile, unlit, once per grid.

## Colour
| Role | Hex | Use | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ember | `#E2571E` | Accent word, quote field | 15% |
| Teal | `#4C7C86` | Field, calm accent | 20% |
| Sand | `#DED3C4` | Paper field | 25% |
| Ink | `#1E1E1E` | Field + type | 30% |
| Fog | `#D5D5D5` | Neutral field | 10% |

Body type is ink on light, white on dark. Teal is never used on sand; ember is never used on teal.

## Typography
- **Headline** — Archivo 700, uppercase, 3–4 lines, line-height 1.1.
- **Support** — Archivo 400, lowercase, three short lines max, line-height 1.6.
- **Kicker** — Archivo 700, .18em, uppercase, 11–12px.
- **Margin note** — handwriting face (Caveat), on torn paper only, never over photography.

Scale (1080×1080): headline 60–80px · support 30px · kicker 24px · note 44px · hashtag 22px.

## Components
- **Taped paper scrap** — cream torn sheet, black tape strip rotated 2°, soft drop shadow, handwritten note inside.
- **Circled phrase** — loose ellipse in ember or ink around the payoff phrase. One per frame.
- **Checklist** — 4–5 rows, square outline boxes, one left unchecked for tension.
- **Numbered pillar list** — ember numerals, ink uppercase labels, 01–05.
- **Hand arrow + asterisk** — curved ember arrow for direction; ✳ as corner mark.
- **Quote tile** — ember field, oversized quote mark, uppercase quote, small attribution.

## Layout
- 1:1 frames, margin 8%. Headline top-left or bottom-left, support beneath, mark in the opposite corner.
- Nine-grid mix: 3 ink, 2 sand, 2 teal, 1 ember, 1 photo-led. Ember tile never in a corner slot.
- Black tape strips may cover list rows to mark completed steps.

## Voice
Calm strategist English, aphoristic: "Confidence is quiet. Insecurities are loud.", "Quality is remembered long after price is forgotten." Support lines are three parallel lowercase fragments.
