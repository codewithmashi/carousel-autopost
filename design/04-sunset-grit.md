# 04 — Sunset Grit

Local social-marketing agency system: high-contrast black-and-white city photography, one hot orange-red, condensed heavy caps, brush script for the emotional word. Reference: SMM Los Angeles story set.

## Principles
- **Three inks only.** Orange-red, black, off-white. Photography desaturated so red never competes.
- **One word in script.** The brush italic takes the payoff word — attention, simple, LA — never a full line.
- **Place is the subject.** Palms, skyline, Hollywood sign, skate culture. Local proof beats stock gloss.
- **Corner triple.** Every post: a kicker, a headline block, and a three-line service stack in a corner.

## Colour
| Role | Hex | Use | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunset red | `#F03A17` | Field + accent word | 30% |
| Night black | `#0C0C0C` | Field, photo base | 35% |
| Bone | `#F5F3F0` | Light field | 25% |
| Pure white | `#FFFFFF` | Type on photo, script on red | 8% |
| Grain grey | `#8A8A8A` | Photo midtone | 2% |

Red type sits on black or bone. On red fields type is black; white on red is reserved for the brush script.

## Typography
- **Headline** — Anton, uppercase, 2–4 stacked lines, tracking −1%, line-height .9.
- **Script accent** — brush marker face, skewed −6°, one word, optionally with a brush underline slab.
- **Kickers / stacks / CTA** — Barlow Condensed 600/700 uppercase, .14–.20em.

Scale (1080×1920 story): hero 150–210px · script 130–170px · kicker 30px · service stack 28px · CTA 26px.

## Components
- **Red-word emphasis** — one or two words of the headline in sunset red.
- **Script + slab** — brush word with a 6px rounded rule beneath.
- **Photo overlay block** — kicker top-left, headline bottom-left, no scrim beyond a soft bottom gradient.
- **Brush zigzag mark** — single energetic stroke, max one per post.
- **Red field post** — black caps headline, white script payoff.
- **Triple-proof list** — three short uppercase lines under a 3px rule.

## Layout
- 4:5 and 9:16 frames, margin 6%. Headlines anchor to one corner; never centred over busy photography.
- Nine-grid mix: 3 red, 3 black/photo, 3 bone, with red placed on a diagonal.
- Cut-out palm silhouettes may break the frame edge on red fields.

## Imagery
Black-and-white LA: downtown skyline at dusk, palm rows, Hollywood sign, skaters, boulevard traffic. Heavy contrast, crushed blacks, visible grain.

## Voice
Short American agency imperatives in caps: "Your brand deserves attention", "Content that drives results", "Let's grow your brand in LA". Proof lines come in threes.
