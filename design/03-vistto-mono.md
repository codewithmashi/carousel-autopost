# 03 — Vistto Mono

Positioning-and-social system built on monochrome archival photography, condensed uppercase questions, and one electric blue used only as an interface artefact. Reference: @vistto.online.

## Principles
- **Ask, don't announce.** Nearly every post is a question aimed at the reader; answers live in the caption.
- **Grayscale, always.** All photography desaturated — objects, archive tech, sculpture, signage.
- **The brand interrupts.** The "SEJA VISTTO." lockup sits inside the headline, mid-sentence, at a fraction of its size.
- **One blue moment.** Blue is a mention bubble or a single field per nine-grid. Interface, not brand colour.

## Colour
| Role | Hex | Use | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black | `#0B0B0B` | Field | 40% |
| Paper white | `#F4F4F2` | Field | 30% |
| Concrete | `#9A9A98` | Photo midtone | 15% |
| Mention blue | `#2B5CE6` | Bubble, one field | 12% |
| Panel | `#1B1B1B` | Cards, signage | 3% |

White on black photography is default. Black type only on paper white or on the blue field.

## Typography
- **Headline** — Barlow Condensed 500/700, uppercase, line-height .95, weight mixed line to line for emphasis.
- **Emphasis word** — Archivo italic 600/700, sentence case, dropped inside the condensed headline.
- **Body / CTA** — Archivo 400 for sub-copy; 700 uppercase .1em for calls to action.
- **Inline lockup** — "SEJA / VISTTO." two lines, 700, 8–10px, .06em, baseline-aligned to the headline's x-height.

Scale (1080×1350): question hero 100–150px · emphasis .85× hero · sub-copy 26px · CTA 22px · lockup 18px.

## Components
- **Mention bubble** — blue chat bubble, 16px radius with one square corner, overlapping the frame edge. One per post.
- **Inverted knockout** — white box, black type: the twist line after a bold quote ("Você leu certo.").
- **Parenthetical aside** — small hairline-boxed or bracketed question under the headline, centred.
- **Signage panel** — headline set inside a photographed sign, screen, badge or billboard.
- **Paper-white tile** — the light beat in a dark grid; key term underlined 3px.
- **Interrupted headline** — lockup wedged between two words of the question.

## Layout
- 4:5 frames. Headline occupies the top ~45%; photography or object fills the rest.
- Handle appears exactly once per post: bubble, corner mono line, or inline lockup.
- Nine-grid mix: 5 dark, 3 light, 1 blue.

## Imagery
Black-and-white archive and studio objects — rotary phones, plaster busts, CRTs, Nokia handsets, brick-phone radios, billboards, laminated badges, paper on car windscreens. Hard directional light, deep shadow.

## Voice
Direct Brazilian Portuguese, provocative and slightly confrontational: "A sua empresa está invisível?", "Seu concorrente não é melhor. Ele só está se posicionando direito." CTAs ask for a DM keyword.
