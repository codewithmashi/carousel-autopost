# 05 — Street Manifesto

Civic-campaign system: hand-painted brush headlines, one tomato red, warm paper, oversized outlined glyphs as watermarks. A public-space voice — direct, spoken, slightly rude. Reference: *al tacho anti-litter campaign.

## Principles
- **Speak street.** Copy is local slang and imperatives, in the voice of someone shouting from the corner.
- **Brush vs. grotesque.** Brush carries the slogan; a neutral grotesque carries facts and data. That contrast is the system.
- **Glyph as texture.** A single huge letter or ✳ sits behind the message, cropped by the frame, outlined in paper or red.
- **Real streets, real proof.** Documentary photos of the intervention itself, with the slogan set into the scene.

## Colour
| Role | Hex | Use | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomato | `#E8381A` | Field + accent | 35% |
| Paper | `#F4F1E8` | Field | 30% |
| Asphalt | `#1E1B18` | Field | 25% |
| Brush white | `#FFFFFF` | Glyph outlines on dark | 8% |
| Dust | `#8E8B80` | Photo midtone | 2% |

Legal pairs: asphalt on paper, paper on asphalt, asphalt on tomato. Red on paper for single words and data highlights only.

## Typography
- **Slogan** — hand-painted brush, uppercase, 2–4 lines, ragged baselines, letters touching.
- **Data / body** — Archivo 400/500; uppercase for data lists, sentence case for paragraphs, line-height 1.35.
- **Signature** — `*al tacho` 600, .12em, 9–13px, asterisk always attached. Hashtags in tomato.

Scale (1080×1080): slogan 120–190px · statement 44–56px · data 34–40px · micro-caption 22px · signature 20px.

## Components
- **Outlined glyph watermark** — a single letter at 250–400% of the frame height, stroke only, cropped behind the slogan.
- **Red-word emphasis** — one term of a neutral statement in tomato.
- **Red arrow** — carousel "next" indicator, bottom-left only.
- **Hand-drawn circle** — loose ellipse around the accusation word.
- **Asphalt verse tile** — four short lines of street verse, red payoff line.
- **Mark-field infographic** — a grid of hand ✕ marks with exactly one red: "un papel no hace nada."

## Layout
- 1:1 for slogans, 4:5 for photography. Margin 8%; data tiles tighten to 6%.
- Signature top-left or top-right; hashtag in the opposite corner.
- Nine-grid mix: 3 red, 3 paper, 2 asphalt, 1 photo. Never two red tiles adjacent.

## Imagery
Street documentary: sidewalks, plane trees, bins, apartment façades, hand-painted slogans stencilled onto the scene itself. Available light, slightly faded colour.

## Voice
Rioplatense Spanish, imperative and colloquial: "Che vos, levantá el papelito", "Toda la basura, al tacho — y si no hay tacho, al bolsillo." Facts stated flatly as equations (material = years).
