04 / Question one

01

Where does the data already live?

If the answer is "someone else's system", you are not building a screen. You are negotiating an integration, on their timeline.

@codewithmashi04/08
05 / 08 · @codewithmashi
05 / Questions two & three

02

How many kinds of user?

One kind is a login. Three kinds is a permissions system.

03

Anything running while the app is closed?

Reminders, sync, anything scheduled. That is a server, not a screen.

06 / Questions four & five

04

Does money move inside the app?

Payments bring refunds, receipts, tax and store rules with them.

05 / And when it breaks

Somebody needs a way to refund, unblock and correct. That admin surface is a second product.
