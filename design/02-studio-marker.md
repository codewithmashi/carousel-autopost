# 02 — Studio Marker

Creative-studio feed system. Cool grey and white, one saturated mustard, documentary photography, marker lettering doing the talking. Reference: die ende® / all-things fashion-tech feed.

## Principles
- **Marker or grotesque, never both loud.** A post is either a marker statement on white or a photo with a tight grotesque headline.
- **Grey is a colour.** Cool grey carries product and object shots and separates type posts from photo posts in grid rhythm.
- **Mustard = attention.** Yellow is a full field or a highlighter, never type colour on white. Max 2 yellow tiles per nine.
- **UI as ornament.** Save marks, carousel dots, AirDrop sheets, detection boxes — borrowed interface parts are the only decoration.

## Colour
| Role | Hex | Use | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mustard | `#FFC800` | Case-study field | 20% |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Type-post field | 40% |
| Cool grey | `#D9D9D9` | Object/product field | 20% |
| Ink | `#111111` | Type, tags | 15% |
| Signal green | `#2E7D46` | Detection UI only | 5% |

Yellow type only on ink or dark photography. Marker lettering is always ink-black on white.

## Typography
- **Statement** — marker script (Permanent Marker as stand-in for a real brush face), 3–5 words, slight baseline drift.
- **Headline** — Anton, uppercase, tight stacked lines, used over photography.
- **Body / labels** — Archivo 400/600/700. Section labels 700 uppercase .06em; captions 400, 1.45 leading, centred under product shots.

Scale (1080×1350): marker hero 130–200px · photo headline 90–120px · label 34px · caption 26px · signature 22px.

## Components
- **Save mark** — 22px square outline, top right, on every post.
- **Highlighter caption** — yellow-behind lines, staggered left edges, over portrait photography.
- **Manila-folder card** — cream folder with tab, 3px offset shadow; recurring "CLIENT WINS" series device.
- **Yellow case-study tile** — inset product photo on mustard, section label above, centred caption below.
- **Detection overlay** — 2px green boxes plus micro readout text on dark photography.
- **Corner tag pair** — ink bar, two mono labels, bottom edge of photo posts.

## Layout
- 4:5 frames, margin 7%, full-bleed for photography.
- Grid rhythm photo · type · photo. Never two marker posts adjacent.
- Each post carries either the save mark or the signature lockup — not both.

## Imagery
Street-shot menswear, studio product on seamless grey, crosswalk candids, blurred storefronts, screen-recording artefacts. Desaturated to neutral; no warm filters.

## Voice
Studio-notebook English, lowercase confidence: "good things take time.", "trust your vision", "it's not that serious". Case-study captions are two flat sentences about form and material.
