Your app shipped nothing this year. Why's the bill still here?

Zero new features doesn't mean zero recurring cost:

→ Hosting, storage and bandwidth bill by usage — not by how many features you shipped.
→ Every OS update Apple or Google ships is a compatibility pass your app has to pass, whether you asked for it or not.
→ Signing certificates expire on their own clock, features or no features.
→ Push, analytics and payment SDKs deprecate on the vendor's schedule — silent until a user hits the dead one.
→ Apple's Developer Program fee — $99/year — renews whether you shipped a release or not.

None of these show up on a feature list. All of them show up on the bill.
