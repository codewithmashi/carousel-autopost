Is account deletion a feature or a store rule?

It's a rule. So are two others founders usually meet for the first time in a rejection email:

→ Users need a real, in-app way to delete their account — a support inbox doesn't count.
→ Your privacy label has to match what the app actually collects, not what the deck promised.
→ Selling digital goods in-app means using the platform's own payment rail.

None of these show up on a feature list. All three show up in review. Put them in the quote before you put them in the backlog.
