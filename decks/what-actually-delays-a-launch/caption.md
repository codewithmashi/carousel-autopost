Your launch is not waiting on code.

Founders plan the part they can picture: the screens, the build, the number of weeks it takes to write them. That part is usually close to right. What blows the date is everything the build sits waiting on — the decision nobody has made, the account nobody can log into, the review queue nobody controls.

A team can build around one open question for a day. By the third they are guessing, and guesses get rebuilt. The signing identity, the real copy, the real logo, the login to the system you already run on: none of that is on the plan, and all of it stops work when it is missing.

The plan counts screens. The calendar counts answers.
