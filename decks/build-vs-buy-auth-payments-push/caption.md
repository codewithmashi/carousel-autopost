No one picks your app for the login screen. Yet login, payments and notifications are where first-time founders spend their first month.

Three tests settle build versus buy before the argument starts. Rent anything regulated — card rules, tax and identity change without asking you, and a vendor tracks those changes for a living. Own what you would defend — if a competitor copied it tomorrow and you would be furious, build it. Price the exit first — before you sign, ask what leaving costs and whether you can export your users and their history.

Integration finishes. Maintenance does not. Rented plumbing is the only part of your app someone else keeps fixing after launch — and that, not the setup week, is what you are actually buying.

Save this and run the three tests before you sign anything.
