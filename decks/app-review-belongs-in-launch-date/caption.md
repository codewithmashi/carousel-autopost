You set the launch date. Review sets the real one.

Nobody schedules around a gate they don't own — but app review doesn't run on your calendar:

→ There's no published, guaranteed review turnaround — most submissions clear in a day or two, some don't.
→ A rejection doesn't hold your spot in the queue — you resubmit and re-enter it.
→ Metadata and screenshot updates get reviewed too, not just the binary.

Put review on the calendar before the build starts, not after it ships.
