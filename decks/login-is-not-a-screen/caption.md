Is "add sign-in" one ticket — or four?

Founders scope it as a screen. What ships is a system:

→ Password reset needs its own token, its own expiry, its own email — not a repurposed login form.
→ Every session, on every device, has to be revocable — not just left to expire on its own.
→ Whatever the account created has to be deletable — Apple and Google both require an in-app path before they'll list the app at all.

None of these are on the login mockup. All of them are on the build.
