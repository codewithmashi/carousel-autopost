Fixed price. Safe for whom?

A fixed price doesn't remove the risk in a mobile build — it prices it. The number carries a quiet buffer for app review, OS updates, the API you don't control, and the twenty things nobody sees until the app is on a real phone. You pay that buffer whether or not the risk ever shows up.

And once it's signed, the only way anyone protects that number is to say no. "That's out of scope" isn't bad faith — it's the contract working exactly as designed.

What to ask for instead: fix a price to the discovery, cap each phase, and re-quote at every boundary with what the last phase taught you. Name the unknowns in the contract — the integration, the store review, the migration — and agree now how each gets priced once it's knowable.

Same change request, mid-build. Fixed price turns it into an argument about the spec. A capped phase turns it into a decision about the next slot.

Save this and read it again before you sign anything.
