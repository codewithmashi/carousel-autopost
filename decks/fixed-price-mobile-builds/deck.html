02 — the problem

The quote is fixed.
The risk is not.

( App review. OS updates. Someone else's API. And the twenty things you only see once the app is on a real phone. )

So the price carries a buffer for all of it — paid whether or not it happens. And after signing, the only way to protect that price is to say no.
