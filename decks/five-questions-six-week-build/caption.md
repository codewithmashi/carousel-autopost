Five questions decide whether your app takes six weeks or six months. Most scoping calls ask none of them.

Where does the data already live? How many kinds of user are there? Does anything have to happen while the app is closed? Does money move inside the app? And who fixes it when it goes wrong?

None of those are technical questions. But each one is either a small answer given in an hour, or a month nobody put in the plan — found halfway into the build, when it is most expensive to find.

Same app, either way. The difference is only whether you asked first.

Save this and ask all five before you sign anything.
