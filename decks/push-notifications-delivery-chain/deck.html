04 — hop one

What happens when the token is stale?

Every install gets a device token that can expire, rotate, or point at an app the user already deleted. Send to a dead one and it vanishes — no error, no retry.

05 — hops two & three

Whose server sits between yours and the phone?

APNs and FCM decide what gets through — they queue, throttle and drop what they don't like, and neither reports back to you.

And is the app even running?
