Most startup MVPs get thrown away inside a year.

Not because the code was bad. Because three decisions got made in week one — by people who didn't know they were making decisions. What counts as scaffolding. What the data model looks like. Which parts you rent instead of build.

Get those right and your MVP becomes version one. Get them wrong and it becomes a rewrite you pay for twice: once to build it, once to replace it.
