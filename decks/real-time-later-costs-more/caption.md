"We'll add real-time later" is the most expensive sentence in a scoping call.

Real-time isn't a feature you bolt on. It's a decision about how your app gets its data — and every screen you've already built assumes the old answer.

You don't have to build chat in week one. You do have to decide in week one which screens will never sit still, and rent the pipe before eight months of code depends on the other shape.

Save this before you agree to "later".
